CiteGuard-Eval is a reproducible benchmark that tests whether AI models can verify academic citation metadata and determine whether a cited source actually supports a research claim. Its ten controlled tasks cover author-order, title, year, venue and DOI mismatches; missing identifiers; unsupported or contradicted claims; partial evidence; causal overclaiming; and combined citation/evidence checks. A deterministic Python grader scores verdicts, issue codes and field-level judgments without using an LLM judge. The included fixtures are synthetic and fully auditable. The project is designed to expose a practical weakness in fluent AI-generated academic writing: confident but incorrect citation validation and evidence attribution.
