CiteGuard-Eval currently has 10 synthetic benchmark tasks.

These tasks examine three important capabilities.

A. Bibliographic verification

Can the AI detect when an academic reference contains incorrect information?

Examples:
- Wrong DOI.
- Incorrect publication year.
- Wrong author order.
- Incorrect article title.
- Missing DOI.

B. Evidence verification

Can the AI distinguish between a claim that is supported by a paper and one that is contradicted by the paper?

Examples:
- Claim says 18% improvement, but evidence reports 2%.
- Claim suggests causation, but the study only establishes association.
- Claim exaggerates what the cited research actually establishes.

C. Uncertainty and abstention

Can the AI admit that it cannot verify something instead of confidently inventing an answer?

Examples:
- Missing DOI.
- Insufficient evidence.
- Unsupported claims.
- Partially supported claims.
